### kicad
KiCad is a free, open-source software suite used for designing printed circuit boards (PCBs). It allows you to create electronic schematics and then convert them into detailed PCB layouts, ready for manufacturing.

A PCB (Printed Circuit Board) is a physical board that connects electronic components using copper tracks instead of wires. It’s the backbone of all modern electronic devices—from smartphones to industrial machines.

KiCad is popular among hobbyists, students, and professionals because it offers powerful features like:
Schematic capture
PCB layout with 3D viewer
Integrated tools for electrical rule checking
Support for multi-layer boards
It's a great tool for turning your electronic ideas into real, functioning hardware.
